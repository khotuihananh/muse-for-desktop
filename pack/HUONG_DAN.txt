MUSE DESKTOP 1.6.4 — HUONG DAN NHANH
====================================

App boc trang muse.ai vao mot cua so desktop thuong tru.
Ban 1.6.0: thanh cong cu gom 3 nut chup + 2 nut quay man hinh,
moi nut gan duoc mot phim tat tuy chon, chup/quay xong dan thang
vao o chat. Che do "nhan Enter de chup" (gan man hinh kieu Google)
da GO HAN — Enter trong o chat hoat dong binh thuong nhu tren web.

3 NUT CHUP TREN THANH CONG CU
- Len .......... Khoanh mot vung tren man hinh (co che len WinLens:
  man hinh lam toi xung quanh, vung khoanh trong veo, chip hien
  kich thuoc pixel that), tha chuot la chup dung vung do va dan
  vao o chat.
- Man hinh 1 ... Chup toan bo man hinh 1, dan vao o chat.
- Man hinh 2 ... Chup toan bo man hinh 2, dan vao o chat.
Ca 3 deu la phim nong toan cuc: dang o app nao bam phim cung chay,
app tu mang cua so Muse ra truoc va dan anh vao o chat.

QUAY MAN HINH (BAN 1.6.0)
- Nut "Quay 1" / "Quay 2": bam lan 1 bat dau quay toan bo man
  hinh tuong ung; bam lan 2 (hoac bam lai dung nut do) dung quay,
  video MP4 duoc dan thang vao o chat.
- Trong luc quay, nut chuyen thanh "Dung mm:ss" dem thoi gian;
  nut quay cua man hinh kia bi khoa (chi quay 1 man 1 luc).
- Quay bang ffmpeg dong goi kem app (gdigrab + H.264), 30 fps.
  Chi quay hinh, KHONG thu tieng.
- File video giu lai tai %LocalAppData%\MuseDesktop\recordings,
  ke ca sau khi da dan vao chat.
- Phim tat cho 2 nut quay: mac dinh CHUA gan — tu gan trong menu
  gan phim (chuot phai vao nut), vi du Ctrl+Alt+F9 / Ctrl+Alt+F10.

GAN PHIM BAT KY CHO CAC NUT
- Mac dinh: Len = Ctrl+Alt+V, Man hinh 1 = Ctrl+Alt+1,
  Man hinh 2 = Ctrl+Alt+2.
- Doi phim: CHUOT PHAI vao mot nut bat ky tren thanh cong cu (hoac
  menu khay he thong > "Gan phim chup..."), bam nut Doi o dong tuong
  ung roi go to hop phim muon dung.
- Gan duoc CA MOT PHIM DON LE bat ky (ban 1.6.1). Chu y: phim
  don le lam phim nong toan cuc se bi app giu o moi chuong trinh
  — vi du gan chu A thi go chu A o noi khac se khong ra chu.
- Cau hinh phim luu tai %LocalAppData%\MuseDesktop\hotkeys.json,
  cac lan mo sau tu ap dung.
- Alt+Space (an / hien cua so) giu co dinh, khong doi duoc.

MENU CHUOT PHAI TRONG TRANG (BAN 1.6.4)
- Chuot phai vao bat ky cho nao trong khung chat: menu rieng cua
  app hien ra gom: 3 lenh chup, 2 lenh quay, gan phim, tai lai
  trang, va muc an/hien thanh cong cu.
- "An thanh cong cu (chi con khung chat)": an ca thanh cong cu
  lan thanh trang thai — cua so chi con trang chat tran, KHONG
  con dai hay chi tiet thua nao. Hien lai cung bang chinh menu
  chuot phai nay (muc "Hien thanh cong cu"), hoac menu khay.
- Thanh tieu de cua so da duoc lam toi mau (dark) theo app.
- An giao dien khong anh huong phim nong: van chup bang phim duoc.

PHIM VA KHAY HE THONG
- Nut X tren cua so: chi an xuong khay (app van chay nen).
- Khay he thong (click phai vao icon M): mo Muse / 3 lenh chup /
  gan phim / an-hien thanh cong cu / tai lai trang / bat-tat khoi
  dong cung Windows / thoat han.
- Click dup vao icon khay: mo Muse.

LAN DAU CHAY
- Dang nhap tai khoan Meta mot lan trong cua so app, app se nho
  dang nhap cho cac lan sau (du lieu luu tai
  %LocalAppData%\MuseDesktop).

GO CAI DAT
- Settings > Apps > Muse Desktop > Uninstall.

TIEU DE CUA SO
- Tieu de ghi so phien ban (Muse Desktop 1.6.4) de chup anh la biet
  ngay dang chay ban nao.
